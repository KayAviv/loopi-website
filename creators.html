Subscription channels

Open a channel and set your price. Subscribers get your channel feed and locked posts; you keep 80% of the subscription revenue after the app stores' fees.

Locked posts

Any post can be locked at a credit price you choose. Fans unlock it with credits; you keep 80% of the unlock revenue after store fees.

Live gifts & tips

Go live and your viewers send gifts and tips in real time. Same rule as everything else on LOOPI: 80% of the gift revenue after store fees is yours.

The split, explained honestly

When a fan pays, Apple or Google takes their store fee first — that's their cut, and no platform can change it. From what remains, 80% goes to you and 20% to LOOPI. There is no second platform cut, no processing deduction, and no "adjustment" line later.

Payouts run weekly, by PayPal or bank transfer, with no minimum balance. If you earned it, it moves.

And if the split ever changes in the future, founding creators keep the better rate for 12 months from the change. In writing, right here.

What you keep control of

  • Your content stays yours. You grant LOOPI a non-exclusive licence to host and show it — and that licence ends when you delete the content.
  • No exclusivity. Post on LOOPI and everywhere else. We'd rather earn your next upload than lock it in.
  • No posting minimums. Daily, weekly, whenever — your schedule is your business.
  • Your prices. You set subscription prices, lock prices and tip amounts inside the app's ranges.

Getting started

Join in three steps

Most creators are set up the same day. Founding creators get personal help from our team.

1. Get the app

Get LOOPI on the App Store, or join the Android closed test on Google Play, and create your account with the handle you want to be known by.

2. Open your channel

From your profile, open your creator channel, set your subscription price, and add your payout details (PayPal or bank). Post your first videos — new posts or your best existing clips.

3. Get paid weekly

Earnings from subscriptions, locked posts and live gifts land in your creator balance and pay out every week. Email cfir@kay-group.net any time — real humans, fast answers.

Founding creator badge

The first creators on LOOPI receive the verified founding badge on their profile and featured placement for at least 12 months. Tell us you're coming and we'll help with your profile, your first posts and your launch.

Referral program

Bring creators. Earn 10%.

When you bring a creator to LOOPI, you earn 10% of what they spend and earn on the platform — and it comes out of LOOPI's share, never out of theirs. Their 80% stays 80%.

There's no cap and no expiry while the program runs. If someone signs up because of you, you share in what they build here.

  • 10% of referred creators' earnings — paid from LOOPI's 20%, not theirs.
  • 10% of referred users' credit spend too — fans you bring count as well.
  • No cap. Refer ten creators or ten thousand.
  • Simple attribution. New users can name who brought them at sign-up; make sure your people know your handle.
