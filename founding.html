GoalJar — Kids Learn Money

Teach your kids money — for two years, or for life

Completing store review

Every parent knows the moment. Your child points at something shiny and says, “Buy it!” — and you hear yourself answer with the same words your parents used: “Money doesn’t grow on trees.” Schools teach our kids math. Almost nobody teaches them money — what it feels like to earn it, to wait for it, to save toward something that matters.

GoalJar changes where the lesson happens. Kids set goals, do chores, earn virtual coins, and watch their jar fill — making real choices, spend now or save for something bigger, in a safe world where mistakes cost nothing and lessons last a lifetime. Parents guide from their own dashboard. No real money, no ads, no tricks — just the habits we all wish we’d learned at eight years old.

Your backing today builds the future these kids will live in: it funds GoalJar’s final development, the school pilot program that puts it into classrooms, and the servers that keep families’ data safe. A child who learns to control money becomes an adult who controls their life.
