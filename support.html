Support

How can we help?

Start with the common fixes below — most issues are solved in a minute. Still stuck? Email us.

Email us: cfir@kay-group.net

We reply within 2 business days — usually much faster. Include your @handle and a short description of the problem so we can help on the first reply. Never send us your password; we'll never ask for it.

Write to support

Account & sign-in

Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and we'll email you a reset code. Check spam if it doesn't arrive in a few minutes.

Reset code not arriving? Make sure you're checking the email tied to your account, wait 5 minutes, then request a fresh code — each new request replaces the old code. Still nothing? Email us from that same address.

Change password, email or handle: Profile → Settings → Account & Security. Handles can be changed once every 30 days. Optional two-factor sign-in by email code is in the same screen.

Delete your account: Profile → Settings → Account & Security → Delete account. Deletion removes your profile and content; some records are kept briefly where the law requires.

Credits & purchases

Buying credits: Open the credits wallet in the app and choose a pack. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, and credits land in your wallet as soon as the store confirms.

Paid but no credits? Fully close and reopen the app first — balances refresh on launch. If the pack still hasn't arrived after an hour, email us your @handle and the store receipt (not your card details).

Refunds: Because purchases run through the App Store or Google Play, refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Credits already spent on gifts, tips, unlocks or AI generation can't be refunded by us.

Remember: credits have no cash value and can't be transferred or cashed out.

Creator payouts

When do I get paid? Creator earnings (subscriptions, locked posts, live gifts) pay out weekly by PayPal or bank transfer, with no minimum balance. Your share is 80% after store fees.

Payout missing? Check that your payout details in the app are complete and match your PayPal email or bank account exactly. A name or email mismatch is the usual cause. Fixed the details and still waiting after 7 days? Email us.

Tax forms: Depending on your country, we may ask for basic tax information before your first payout. We'll only ever ask through the app or from our official email.

Reporting content & safety

Report a video, post, comment or account: Tap the ••• menu on the content and choose Report. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team, and content that breaks the rules is removed. Uploads and AI generations are also screened automatically before they go public.

Block someone: Open their profile → ••• → Block. They won't be able to follow you, message you or see your private content.

Something urgent — threats, exploitation, or content involving a minor: report in the app and email us with "URGENT" in the subject. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Copyright: If content on LOOPI uses your work without permission, email us with links and proof of ownership and we'll act on valid takedown requests.

Video & app problems

Videos won't play or keep freezing: Check your connection, then fully close and reopen the app. If a specific video never loads while others do, it may still be processing or under review — try again in a little while.

Upload stuck? Large files on a weak connection are the usual cause. Connect to Wi-Fi, keep the app open, and retry. Videos are capped at 60 seconds.

Calls not connecting: Allow camera and microphone permissions for LOOPI in your phone's settings, and make sure both people are on the latest version of the app.

Live streaming

Can't go live? Live streaming needs camera/microphone permission and a stable connection. If your account is very new or was recently restricted for rule violations, live access may be paused — email us if you think that's a mistake.

Gifts: You must be 18 or older to buy credits and send gifts. Gifts are final once sent. Creators receive 80% of gift revenue after store fees, paid weekly.
