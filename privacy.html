Privacy Policy

How LOOPI handles your information — in plain language, with the details kept honest.

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 18, 2026

LOOPI ("LOOPI", "we", "us") is a short-video application operated by 2445495 Alberta Ltd., doing business as Kay Group ("Kay Group"), with a mailing address at 111 Henderson Ave, Thornhill, Ontario, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the LOOPI mobile apps and this website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. By using LOOPI you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use LOOPI.

1. Who can use LOOPI

LOOPI is rated 18+. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account and to use features such as live gifting. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will remove it. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

2. Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address, password (stored in hashed form — we never store or see your plain password), handle, display name, date of birth where requested, and profile details you add, such as your photo, bio and website link.
  • Phone number (optional): if you choose to add and verify a phone number, we store the number and a "verified" flag so other users who already have your number can find you. Phone verification is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google), which processes the number to send and confirm the SMS code.
  • Your content: videos, photos, stories, captions, comments, messages, live streams, and AI prompts and generations you create with LOOPI, plus the metadata the app attaches to them (such as when they were posted and the audience you chose).
  • Contacts (optional): if you use "Find contacts", the app compares your contacts' phone numbers against verified LOOPI accounts on our servers at the time of the search. We do not store your address book, and numbers that don't match an account are not retained.
  • Purchases and payouts: if you buy credits or subscriptions, the payment is processed by Apple or Google; we receive confirmation of the purchase and a transaction reference, not your card number. If you are a creator, we collect the payout details you provide (such as your PayPal email or bank details) and any tax information the law requires, so we can pay you.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email or through support, and your responses to surveys or promotions.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version, language, and a push-notification token so we can deliver notifications you allow.
  • Usage information: the videos you watch, like, comment on, save and share; who you follow; searches; and basic interaction events. We use this to run the feeds (For You, Following, Nearby), show your watch history to you, count views, and improve the app.
  • Approximate location: if you post publicly to the discovery map or use Nearby, we use an approximate area (city-level or a coarse grid), never your exact location. Location is only used when you choose features that need it, and the map never shows precise positions.
  • Log and security data: IP address, timestamps, crash reports and sign-in events, used to operate, secure and debug the service.
  • Cookies on this website: this website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. A static host may keep standard server logs (IP address, page requested, time) for security and operation.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide LOOPI: accounts, feeds, posting, stories, chat, calls, live streaming, games, sports scores, AI generation, credits, subscriptions and payouts.
  • To personalize your feed and suggestions (such as "People you may know", based on mutual follows and verified contacts).
  • To moderate content and enforce our Terms: uploads and AI generations may be screened automatically, and reported content is reviewed by our team.
  • To communicate with you: service emails (password resets, security codes), notifications you enable, and replies to your support requests.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and illegal activity, and to comply with legal obligations.
  • To understand how LOOPI is used in aggregate and make it better.

We do not sell your personal information. LOOPI carries no advertising, and we do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Who we share information with

  • Other users, as you direct: your public profile (handle, display name, photo, bio, link), public posts and stories, follower counts, and anything you send in chats or show on live streams is visible as the app presents it. Private accounts share posts only with approved followers. Private (vaulted) chats are visible only to their participants; we do not open them as a feature, though content may be accessed where strictly required for safety investigations or by law.
  • Service providers who process information only to run LOOPI for us, under contract: cloud hosting and database (Render), media storage (Backblaze), live audio/video infrastructure (LiveKit), automated content moderation (Sightengine), phone verification and push notifications (Google Firebase), and app distribution and payments (Apple and Google). Our email is handled by Google Workspace.
  • Payment and payout partners: Apple, Google, PayPal, banks and payment processors, to complete purchases and creator payouts.
  • Legal and safety: where required by law, court order or lawful request; to enforce our Terms; or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, creators, Kay Group or the public.
  • Business transfers: if Kay Group or LOOPI is involved in a merger, sale or reorganization, information may transfer with the business, subject to this Policy or a successor policy that is no less protective.

6. How long we keep information

We keep account information and your content while your account is active. When you delete content, it is removed from public view and deleted from our active systems within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited records (for example, transaction and payout records for tax and accounting, moderation records, and backups that cycle out on a fixed schedule). When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, subject to those legal retention duties.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, access controls, and optional two-factor sign-in by email code. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe your account has been compromised, change your password in Settings → Account & Security and contact us immediately.

8. Your choices

  • Profile and posts: edit or delete them any time in the app; switch between a public and private account; choose the audience per post; turn map visibility off per post.
  • Phone and contacts: adding a phone number and using Find contacts are optional, and you can remove your phone number in Settings → Personal details → Phone.
  • Notifications: control push notifications in the app and in your device settings.
  • Account deletion: Profile → Settings → Account & Security → Delete account.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Canada's PIPEDA, and the GDPR/UK GDPR or US state laws where they apply), you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to certain processing, and receive a copy in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, email cfir@kay-group.net from the email address on your account. We will verify your identity and respond within the time the applicable law requires (generally 30 days). You may also complain to your local privacy regulator; in Canada, that's the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

10. International transfers

Kay Group is in Canada. Our service providers may process and store information in Canada, the United States and other countries where they operate. Where information is transferred internationally, we use providers whose practices are consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

11. Changes to this Policy

If we make material changes, we will post the updated Policy here with a new effective date and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email before the changes take effect. Continuing to use LOOPI after the effective date means you accept the updated Policy.

12. Contact us

Privacy questions or requests: cfir@kay-group.net
