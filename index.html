Everything in one app

Made for watching. Built for creating.

LOOPI brings the parts of social video you actually use into one clean app.

Short videos & photo posts

Full-screen videos that just keep flowing, plus photo posts when one frame says it all. Follow people, explore For You and Following feeds, and save the ones you love.

Chat, voice & video calls

Real conversations, not comment threads. Message friends, start group chats, share videos in one tap, and jump on a voice or video call without leaving the app.

Live streaming with gifts

Go live in seconds. Viewers chat in real time, send gifts and tips, and subscribe to your channel — while you keep the stage.

Creator subscriptions

Creators open a subscription channel and share locked posts with their biggest fans. Creators keep 80% of what they earn — after store fees, with no other platform cut surprises.

AI video generation

Type an idea, get a video. LOOPI's built-in AI studio turns a text prompt into a ready-to-post clip, and the AI Remove tool cleans unwanted objects out of your photos.

Games arcade

Quick-match mini-games live right inside LOOPI. Challenge a friend or get matched instantly, and keep the chat going while you play.

Live sports scores

A built-in sports channel with live scores, so the match is always one tap away from the videos everyone will be talking about after.

Nearby & map discovery

See what's being posted around you. The Nearby feed and map view surface public posts by area — never exact locations — so you can find your city's creators.

Credits, gifts & tips

One simple credit wallet powers gifts, tips, locked posts and AI creations across the whole app. Buy a pack once and spend it anywhere on LOOPI.

Questions

Frequently asked

The short answers. For anything else, our support page is one tap away.

Is LOOPI really free?
Yes — downloading LOOPI and watching is free. LOOPI earns from optional credit packs, creator subscriptions, live gifts and Paid Verified. If you never buy anything, you still get the full feed.
How do creators make money on LOOPI?
Three ways: subscription channels, locked posts that fans unlock with credits, and gifts and tips during live streams. Creators keep 80% of that revenue after the app stores' fees, paid out weekly by PayPal or bank transfer with no minimum. See the creators page for the full offer.
What are credits?
Credits are LOOPI's in-app currency. You can buy credit packs in the app and spend credits on live gifts, tips, locked posts and AI video generation. Credits have no cash value and can't be cashed out — they're for spending inside LOOPI.
Can I post photos, or is it video only?
Both. Post short videos or photos, add them to your stories, and choose per post whether it's public, followers-only, or shown on the discovery map.
Is LOOPI for kids?
No. LOOPI is rated 18+. You must be 18 or older to use live gifting, and content is moderated — uploads and AI generations are screened, and anything that breaks the rules is removed.
I'm a creator. How do I join?
Download the app, create your account, and open your channel from your profile — or email us at cfir@kay-group.net and we'll help you set up, including the founding creator badge if you're early.
