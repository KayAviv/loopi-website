How it works

From idea to the stores, in four steps

A defined process, a written quote, and test builds on your own phone as we go — no surprises at the end.

Tell us the idea

Complete the two-minute order form — what your app should do, and who it is for. Email works too.

Receive a written quote

Our team reviews your idea, asks anything we need, and replies with the scope, a fixed price and a delivery date in writing. The price does not change unless the scope does — and scope changes are quoted first, too.

We build, you test

You install real test builds on your own phone throughout the build and tell us what to adjust. Payments are staged against milestones, so you are never paying for promises.

Launch

Final delivery of your app, with store submission handled with you — Google Play and the App Store — and our team available for updates after launch.
