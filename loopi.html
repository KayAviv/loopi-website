What's inside

Everything a short-video app should be — plus a few things it usually isn't

One app for the whole loop: post a video, jump into a live room, keep the conversation going in chat, then challenge your friends to a game.

Short videos, zero fuss

Upload clips up to 10 minutes and scroll a feed that never sleeps — new posts appear live, the moment they're published.

Live streaming & gifts

Go live in one tap. Viewers chat along in real time and send gifts — from a rose to a full dragon — to the streamers they love.

Chat & voice messages

Direct messages, open rooms and password-protected private rooms. Send voice messages up to a minute — they arrive with an automatic written transcript.

A games hub inside

Penalty kicks, ping pong, Volfield duels, FoodNinja and free-coin poker. Start a game session right inside a group chat and climb the standings board.

Add a song

Pick a track from the built-in music library when you post, and LOOPI mixes it onto your video for you — no editing app needed.

Create with AI

No camera roll? Describe the clip and generate an AI video in the app instead.

Creator subscriptions

Creators run their own subscription channels — fans subscribe with credits and unlock the creator's private page and subscriber-only posts, so their biggest fans can support them directly inside LOOPI.

Find your people

Search videos, people, rooms and hashtags, explore a grid of creators you don't follow yet, and pick your interests on day one so the feed starts as yours.

Made to be welcoming

Public or private accounts, follow requests, and reporting and blocking tools — a feed that stays fun for everyone.
