How it works

From idea to the stores, in five steps

Every project starts with a written quote — fixed scope, fixed price, delivery date — before any building begins. Payments are staged against milestones, so you're never paying for vapour.

Tell us the idea

Fill in the two-minute order form — what your app should do, and who it's for. (Email works too.)

Five quick questions

We ask five short questions (five minutes of your time) so we understand the job properly.

A written quote

You get the scope, the fixed price and the delivery date in writing. The price doesn't change unless the scope does — and scope changes get quoted first, too.

We build, you test

You install real test builds on your own phone as we go and tell us what to change. Nothing is a surprise at the end.

Launch

Final delivery of your app, plus store submission handled with you — Google Play and the App Store.
