Packages

Four starting points — quoted individually

Pick the one closest to your idea in the form below. Not sure? Pick “Not sure yet” and the quote will point you the right way.

Starter

One platform, done properly — a focused first app or single-purpose tool.

  • Android or iOS
  • The core screens your idea needs
  • Your branding, colours and content
  • Store submission handled with you

Standard

Both stores and the full essentials — the right home for most business apps.

  • Android and iOS from one build
  • User accounts and sign-in
  • Notifications and content you can update
  • Listings, screenshots and launch handled end to end

Premium

The works — for platforms, marketplaces and apps with real machinery inside.

  • Everything in Standard
  • Payments, chat or live features
  • Maps and third-party integrations
  • An admin view of your app's data

Branded Mini-Game

Your brand, playable — a game of your own for promotions, events and fun.

  • Puzzle, quiz or arcade style
  • Your branding, content and links
  • Ready to share with your customers
  • Can live inside your app, too
